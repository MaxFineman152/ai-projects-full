# Analysis

## Layer 3, Head 2 - Determiners

This layer and head appears to be strongly relating nouns to preceeding determiners. "path", "shop", "table" and "[MASK]" in sentence 2 paid strong attention to only the most recent determiner. Adjectives affecting these nouns also paid significant attention to the most recent determiner.
Other words in the sentence in this layer and head did not pay significant attention to determiners.

Example Sentences:
- "We quickly ran down the long narrow path and went [MASK] the shop."
- "The [MASK] jumped onto the table."

## Layer 10, Head 4 - Prepositions

This layer and head appears to be strongly relating noun phrases (nouns or nouns with determiners) to preceeding perpositions. "the [MASK]" and "the fence" paid strong attention to the prepositions "over" and "under" respectively. Other words in the sentence in this layer and head did not pay significant attention to the prepositions.

Example Sentences:
- "You must go over the [MASK]."
- "I ducked [MASK] under the fence."
